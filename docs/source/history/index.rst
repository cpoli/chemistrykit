History
=======

A chronology of the foundational breakthroughs behind each subpackage,
each one linked back to the corresponding implementation in the code.

.. grid:: 1 2 3 3
   :gutter: 2

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.kinetics
      :link: /history/kinetics_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Integrated rate laws, the Arrhenius equation, Michaelis-Menten enzyme kinetics, and reaction-network dynamics.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.thermo
      :link: /history/thermo_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Equations of state, phase boundaries, reaction equilibrium, and Raoult's/Henry's law mixtures.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.solutions
      :link: /history/solutions_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      pH/pOH and acid-base equilibria, buffers, titration curves, and solubility/activity coefficients.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.md
      :link: /history/md_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Molecular dynamics and force fields: the Lennard-Jones fluid, bonded potentials, and thermostats.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.statmech
      :link: /history/statmech_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Statistical mechanics of molecules: partition functions, the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution, and lattice-gas adsorption.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.quantum
      :link: /history/quantum_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Quantum chemistry: particle-in-a-box, the harmonic oscillator and Morse potential, the rigid rotor, and Huckel theory.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.spectro
      :link: /history/spectro_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Spectroscopy: Beer-Lambert absorbance, rotational and vibrational spectra, Franck-Condon progressions, and NMR multiplets.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.structure
      :link: /history/structure_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Molecular structure and bonding: VSEPR geometry, point-group determination, bond order, and formal charge.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.electrochem
      :link: /history/electrochem_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Electrochemistry: the Nernst equation, standard reduction potentials, Butler-Volmer kinetics, and battery discharge.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.photochem
      :link: /history/photochem_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Photochemistry: Jablonski-diagram kinetics, quantum yields, Stern-Volmer quenching, and photostationary states.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.surface
      :link: /history/surface_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Surface chemistry and catalysis: Langmuir/Freundlich/BET isotherms and Langmuir-Hinshelwood kinetics.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.polymer
      :link: /history/polymer_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Polymer chemistry: chain statistics, molecular-weight distributions, and step-/chain-growth kinetics.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.crystal
      :link: /history/crystal_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Crystallography and solid-state chemistry: crystal systems, packing, lattice energy, and powder XRD.

   .. grid-item-card:: chemistrykit.analytical
      :link: /history/analytical_breakthroughs
      :link-type: doc

      Analytical chemistry: redox/EDTA titrations, chromatographic plate theory, and calibration curves.
