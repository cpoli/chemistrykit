chemistrykit.crystal
========================

The 7 crystal systems and general unit-cell volume; hard-sphere packing
(packing fraction, coordination number, atoms per cell) for SC, BCC,
FCC, and ideal HCP; ionic-crystal lattice energy via the Born-Lande and
Kapustinskii equations, backed by a genuinely converging (Evjen-method)
numerical Madelung constant for NaCl; Bragg's law and powder-XRD peak
positions with structure factors and systematic absences for cubic
lattices; and Schottky/Frenkel point-defect equilibrium.

See the full gallery: :doc:`/api/gallery/crystal/index`.
