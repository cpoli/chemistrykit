Titration curves
------------------

Potentiometric redox (Nernst) and EDTA complexometric titration curves,
with equivalence points located by steepest change or by a Gran plot.

At half-equivalence E=E^\circ_1 exactly, at twice the equivalence volume E=E^\circ_2, and the endpoint is read, as in the laboratory, at the steepest rise of E.">
