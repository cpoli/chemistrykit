Constraints
------------

Rigid bond-length constraints enforced with the SHAKE algorithm, removing
fast bond vibrations from a molecular-dynamics trajectory.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Ryckaert, Ciccotti, and Berendsen&#x27;s SHAKE algorithm (1977) replaces stiff bond springs by exact length constraints, restored after every Verlet position update by iterative corrections along the old bond vectors (~chemistrykit.md.systems.constraints.shake). A water-like triatomic is simulated twice: with stiff harmonic O-H springs (~chemistrykit.md.systems.pair_potentials.HarmonicMolecule), and with the two bonds held rigid by SHAKE (~chemistrykit.md.systems.constraints.ShakeMolecule), keeping the same flexible angle-bending term. Both runs use the same time step, chosen comfortably for the slow bending motion but too long for the fast bond stretch.">
