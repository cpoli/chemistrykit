Step-growth kinetics
-----------------------

The Carothers equation relating the number-average degree of
polymerization to the extent of reaction, and its generalization to a
stoichiometric imbalance between functional groups.

Also the Flory-Stockmayer and Carothers gel points of branching
polymerizations.

For self-condensation of an A_f monomer, Flory (1941) and Stockmayer (1943) found the weight-average degree of polymerization \bar X_w=(1+p)/(1-(f-1)p) (~chemistrykit.polymer.systems.gelation.branching_weight_average_DP) diverges at p_c=1/(f-1) (~chemistrykit.polymer.systems.gelation.flory_stockmayer_gel_point) while the number average (~chemistrykit.polymer.systems.gelation.branching_number_average_DP) stays finite.
