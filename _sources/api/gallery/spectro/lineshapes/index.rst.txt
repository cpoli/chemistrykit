Spectral lineshapes
---------------------

The Gaussian, Lorentzian, and Voigt lineshapes, and the two physically
distinct broadening mechanisms (Doppler/inhomogeneous vs.
lifetime-collisional/homogeneous) that produce them.

    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Compares the three standard spectral lineshapes at matched FWHM: a Lorentzian (the shape a classically radiating damped oscillator, or a finite-lifetime quantum transition, produces -- &quot;homogeneous&quot; broadening, identical for every molecule in the sample), a Gaussian (the shape a Maxwell-Boltzmann spread of line-of-sight velocities produces via the Doppler effect -- &quot;inhomogeneous&quot; broadening, different molecules contributing different, momentarily Doppler-shifted, sub-lines), and their convolution, the Voigt profile, which is what a real spectrum typically shows once both mechanisms contribute together. The Lorentzian has visibly heavier tails than the Gaussian at the same FWHM -- the qualitative signature used to diagnose which mechanism dominates a real measured line.">
