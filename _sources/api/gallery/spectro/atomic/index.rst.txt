Atomic line spectra
--------------------

Discrete atomic lines as chemical fingerprints: Fraunhofer's dark solar
lines, Kirchhoff and Bunsen's flame emission and absorption spectra, and
the Balmer-Rydberg formula for the hydrogen series.
