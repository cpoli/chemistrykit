    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Copper(II) binds ammonia one ligand at a time, Cu(NH_3)_{n-1}^{2+} + NH_3 \rightleftharpoons Cu(NH_3)_n^{2+}, with stepwise constants K_1 &gt; K_2 &gt; K_3 &gt; K_4. Bjerrum showed that the whole ladder follows from the free-ligand concentration alone. The fractions \alpha_n (~chemistrykit.solutions.complex_fractions) and the mean number of bound ligands \bar n (~chemistrykit.solutions.average_ligand_number, Bjerrum&#x27;s formation function) are functions of p[NH_3] only. His &quot;half-\bar n&quot; rule runs the logic backwards: where \bar n = n - \tfrac12, p[NH_3] \approx \log K_n, so the constants can be read off a measured formation curve. The stepwise constants below are of the size Bjerrum measured for Cu(II)-ammonia.">
