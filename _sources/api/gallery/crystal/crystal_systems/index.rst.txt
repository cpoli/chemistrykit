Crystal systems
-----------------

Classifying a unit cell into one of the 7 crystal systems from its
lattice parameters and the general unit-cell-volume formula; Steno's
constant interfacial angles; Miller indices from Hauy's rational face
intercepts; and the 14 Bravais lattices.
