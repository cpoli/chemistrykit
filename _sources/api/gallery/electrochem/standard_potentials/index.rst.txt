    <div class="sphx-glr-thumbcontainer" tooltip="Daniell&#x27;s 1836 two-fluid cell separated a zinc electrode in zinc (or acid) solution from a copper electrode in copper sulfate. Copper metal, not hydrogen gas, is deposited at the cathode, so the cell does not polarize. This example reproduces its standard potential with standard_cell_potential, balances the overall reaction with balance_redox_reaction, and uses the Nernst equation to show how gently the voltage falls as the cell discharges -- the steadiness that made it the first practical voltage standard.">
