Mixtures and colligative properties
------------------------------------

Raoult's-law P-x-y diagrams for an ideal binary solution, the
colligative properties (freezing-point depression, boiling-point
elevation, osmotic pressure), and Henry's law and Raoult's law shown as
the two limiting laws of the same real solution.

But it is tangent to two different straight lines at its two ends: Henry&#x27;s law (~chemistrykit.thermo.systems.mixtures.henry_law_pressure), with slope K_H, as the solute becomes infinitely dilute, and Raoult&#x27;s law (~chemistrykit.thermo.systems.mixtures.raoult_vapor_pressure), with slope P_B^* (the pure-liquid vapor pressure), as the &quot;solute&quot; becomes the only thing present. A simple quadratic interpolation between the two limits, built only to match both slopes exactly, illustrates the textbook picture without needing any real non-ideal-mixture model.">
