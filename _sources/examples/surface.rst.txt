chemistrykit.surface
========================

Langmuir, Freundlich, and BET adsorption isotherms, each with its
standard linearization for fitting parameters from data (BET verified
numerically to reduce to Langmuir as :math:`P_0\to\infty` at fixed
:math:`K=C/P_0`); Langmuir-Hinshelwood single- and dual-site
surface-reaction kinetics; and a turnover-frequency/rate-enhancement
catalysis model built on :mod:`chemistrykit.kinetics`'s Arrhenius
equation.

See the full gallery: :doc:`/api/gallery/surface/index`.
