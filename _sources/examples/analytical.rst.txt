chemistrykit.analytical
========================

Redox and complexometric (EDTA) titration-curve simulation with
equivalence-point detection, alongside the acid-base titrations already
provided by ``chemistrykit.solutions``; chromatographic plate theory and
the van Deemter equation (resolution, selectivity); linear-regression
calibration curves with IUPAC-convention limits of detection/
quantitation; and propagation-of-uncertainty formulas plus Dixon's
Q-test for outlier rejection.

See the full gallery: :doc:`/api/gallery/analytical/index`.
