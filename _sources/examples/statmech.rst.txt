chemistrykit.statmech
=======================

Translational/rotational/vibrational partition functions and the
thermodynamic functions (U, S, Cv) derived from them (including the
Sackur-Tetrode translational entropy); the Maxwell-Boltzmann speed
distribution; and a canonical-ensemble lattice-gas adsorption model (a
statistical-mechanical derivation of the Langmuir isotherm).

See the full gallery: :doc:`/api/gallery/statmech/index`.
