Sections
--------

- **equations_of_state** -- the ideal gas law, van der Waals continuity
  of liquid and gas, Redlich-Kwong, Peng-Robinson vapor pressures, and
  Lewis fugacity.
- **phase_equilibria** -- the Clausius-Clapeyron vapor-pressure curve and
  the Gibbs phase rule.
- **equilibrium** -- the law of mass action, Gibbs-energy minimization,
  the van't Hoff plot, and Le Chatelier's principle.
- **thermochemistry** -- Hess's law and Nernst's heat theorem.
- **mixtures** -- Raoult's-law P-x-y diagrams, Henry's law, van't Hoff's
  osmotic pressure, and Margules activity coefficients.
