Variational H2+, the LCAO method, and Hartree-Fock SCF
--------------------------------------------------------

The one-electron H2+ molecular ion in a small Gaussian basis: LCAO
bonding and antibonding orbitals, bonding density, the Born-Oppenheimer
potential curve, Gaussian-type orbital integrals, the Roothaan-Hall
secular equation ``HC=SCE``, and the shared electron pair; then
closed-shell restricted Hartree-Fock SCF for HeH+ and H2, and the
STO-3G contracted-Gaussian basis.
